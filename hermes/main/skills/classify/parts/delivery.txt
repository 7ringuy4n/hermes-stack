DELIVERY POLICY
target_channel contains only a human-readable destination name. Never place a destination, internal id, platform prefix, or server path inside message or instructions.

For scheduled delivery:
- verbatim means the stored characters are sent unchanged.
- process means the assistant must look up, reason about, or generate the result at fire time.
- transform means the assistant rewrites or summarizes supplied material at fire time without an external lookup.

For an immediate send with no future timing, use normal interactive delivery, response_mode confirm, process_original_message false, and skill_action deliver. Preserve the body exactly. A future or recurring send is always a schedule.
